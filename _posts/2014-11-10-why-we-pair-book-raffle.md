I want to know a little bit more about the reasoning behind Pairprogramming usage in development teams.

To get you, dear readers, tweeting a lot about it I decided to give away an edition of “Pair Programming Illuminated” by Laurie Williams and Robert Kessler. It is the defacto standard book in this field of agile development.

## \#whywepair

![Laurie Williams - Pairprogramming Illuminated](/assets/posts/why-we-pair-book-raffle/51TXKD0A6VL._SX258_BO1-204-203-200_.jpg)

In order to win the book you need to tweet a reason for the use of Pairprogramming in your projects and development teams containing the hashtag **\#whywepair** until Monday the 17.11.2014 (the start date of the [Hamburg Pairprogramming Workshop](https://www.xing.com/events/pairprogramming-workshop-1465057/)).

Its a \#winwin: The participants get some motivational tweets on why developers use Pairprogramming and the tweeting folks get a chance to win the book which provides good advice, even for very seasoned pairing setups.

Let the games begin and let the world know \#whywepair so that other can draw some motivation from it.
