**What it is:** Brainstorming concrete actions or experiments to address the specific interaction challenges identified during analysis.

**Why it matters:** Moves beyond identifying problems to generating potential solutions tailored to how your pair actually works together.

**Think about it:** Based on a recent pairing session, what's one specific experiment you could try next time to improve that interaction (e.g., using timed role switches, explicitly explaining rationale)?
