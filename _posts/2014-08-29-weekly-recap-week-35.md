Following the [practice](http://martinwolf.org/category/weekly-recap/) of [Martin Wolf](http://martinwolf.org/martin-wolf/), I am now posting a recap of things I do over the course of a week. So you can see not only what is on my mind, but what I am actually doing

**Backbone.js **

- backbone inheritance. Looks easy, but there are quite many ways to do it.

  [“Backbone.js & inherticance”](http://www.erichynds.com/blog/backbone-and-inheritance) gives you a intro

- And if you are looking how to inherit those defaults you might wanna see this

  [Stackoverflow thread](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/14614582/backbone-js-model-inheritance)

- Want to validate backbone models? I seem to come back to

  [“backbone.validation”](https://github.com/thedersen/backbone.validation) again and again

- You might wanna check the common pitfalls early on

  “[avoiding common pitfalls](http://ozkatz.github.io/avoiding-common-backbonejs-pitfalls.html)”

Development

- I am a big fan of Grunt.js but I must admit: Gulp is awesome. I got really tired of big configuration files that seemed to be a mistery after a while. You might wanna read the

  “[quick intro to gulp.js](https://www.codefellows.org/blog/quick-intro-to-gulp-js)” and give it a chance. You will not regret it. Lean and Mean I say.

Blogposts

- Olaf Lewitz has a piece on happiness, money and a interview with the CEO of Krankikom GmbH

  “[The most important things in life](http://trustartist.com/2014/08/29/most-important-things-in-life/)”. This was my blogpost of the week.
