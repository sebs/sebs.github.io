I had to do some research for my workshop on BDD workshop and ended up with a big number of videos and podcasts. I thought I’d share that with you here.

What is BDD? Let [Wikipedia](http://www.wikiwand.com/en/Behavior-driven_development) answer this for you:

> In software engineering, behavior-driven development (abbreviated BDD) is a software development process based on test-driven development (TDD).Behavior-driven development combines the general techniques and principles of TDD with ideas from domain-driven design and object-oriented analysis and design to provide software development and management teams with shared tools and a shared process to collaborate on software development.

[Herding code podcast](http://herdingcode.com/episode-42-scott-bellware-on-bdd-and-lean-development/) gives a good start on BDD and a intro on the processes.

[TDD, BDD, And All of the other DDs](http://blogs.telerik.com/agile-blog/posts/12-06-22/podcast-tdd-bdd-and-all-of-the-other-dd-s-with-ted-neward-jessica-kerr-and-glenn-block) should help you distinct the specific test techniques.

[BDD vs TDD explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mT8QDNNhExg) provides a good intro if you are coming from TDD

[BDD - Behavioural Driven Development](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fq44GjdXQ8M) gives a good intro to the outside in approach

The [Interview with Dan North and Liz Keogh](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qWsnmx45734&list=PLmkihKfSoSQDBITqlkEIy2_nmyYua7Wsa) will give you a deeper look into the reasoning behind BDD.

Coming from the Java side of things Gordon Force explains [Behavior Driven Development](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vrry-I4NXCI)

A short [interview with Chris Matts](http://www.infoq.com/interviews/matts-commitment-bdd) on infoQ with a hint to feature injection

A talk by Chris Matts on [Feature Injection](http://vimeo.com/40028722) which I consider very close related to BDD (It is part of my workshop)

The technical Javascript side of [BDD with cucumber.js](http://vimeo.com/51529597) explained by Julien Biezemans

BDD on Puppet/Server setups? Here you go with [Tom Sulston](http://www.infoq.com/presentations/BDD-with-Puppet-Cucumber)

[Liz Keogh on BDD and Cynefin](http://www.infoq.com/interviews/keogh-BDD-cynefin). She is a first mover in this area and certailnly a source you should listen to.

I hope this keeps you entertained. If you have podcast or video sources to add. Feel free to do so in the comments section.
