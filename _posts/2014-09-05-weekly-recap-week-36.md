This week was full of interesting stuff \* COACHING: I had to do some research on feedback types and styles. I stumbled on this document\[, giving a little more background \](http://www.ascd.org/publications/books/108019/chapters/Types-of-Feedback-and-Their-Purposes.aspx)on the special properties of feedback. \* TRAINING: Another research task was on the basics of lean. I am hammering together a workshop on the Lean Basics and Kanban, so I needed \[all in one Place on this Cheatsheet\](http://www.dummies.com/how-to/content/lean-for-dummies-cheat-sheet.html). I need to recreate this with CC. \* FUNDING: I did choose a website Theme and took a deeper look into the Onepager from \[http://www.rebelatheartpr.com/\](http://www.rebelatheartpr.com/) which will serve as a good example for structure and approach. ;) My \[theme of choice\](http://gridgum.com/themes/gw-a-personal-bootstrap-theme/). \* CODING: If you do anything tech related to web and did not read the HTTP Status Codes RFC, \[I urge you to do so. It is !IMPORTANT\](http://www.w3.org/Protocols/rfc2616/rfc2616-sec10.html) \* NEWS: Mytaxi was sold to Daimler. I am applauding the team + Nic Mewes and Sven Külper. A german startup dream come true I guess. That is something that other big german funders never did: Building something original, as one of the first movers, not cloning anything. \[You better worship that\](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/daimler-acquires-transportation-apps-ridescout-140054526.html) ;)))) \* CODING: A HTML/JS based editor. Smalleditor got my attention. I like how it is made. Definetly something to look into. \[https://github.com/jdkanani/smalleditor\](https://github.com/jdkanani/smalleditor) Lots of different things. Interesting times.
