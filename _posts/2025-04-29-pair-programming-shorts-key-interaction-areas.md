**What it is:** Successful pairing involves focusing on specific interaction areas beyond just coding, such as Improving Design, Sharing Knowledge, Coordinating Steps, Tracking Progress, Generating Insights, and Planning Strategy.

**Why it matters:** Intentionally focusing on *how* pairs interact in these areas helps teams leverage pairing more effectively to achieve desired outcomes like better quality and increased resilience.

**Think about it:** Which interaction area presents the biggest challenge or opportunity for your pairing practice right now?
