**What it is:** Examining the specific techniques and *interaction patterns* your pair uses to achieve a particular goal (e.g., how do you propose vs challenge design ideas?).

**Why it matters:** Identifying gaps or ineffective interaction patterns (like unclear proposals or lack of constructive disagreement) pinpoints specific areas for improvement.

**Think about it:** What specific interaction patterns are helping or hindering you in your paring effort?
