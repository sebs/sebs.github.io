1. Identify existing, non-AI, Skill-Gaps and focus on closing them
* identify and make them transparent to the team
* internal trainings
* Small, short term, focus learning groups

2. Organize a culture of Skill Aquisition and sharing
* Improve Pairprogramming
* Hands-On Mentoring as Default
* Shadowing

3. Deliberate Practice
* Contribute and Improve Open Source Projects
* Build things that are 'larger than life' and seem out of reach

4. Playful approach to strategic thinking
* Chess and Go are great to foster strategic thinking and helps with enlarging attention spans
